GOOD QUESTIONS

A little more
clarity.

What is a grind?+

Grinds are extra lessons with a tutor, often one-to-one or in small groups. They’re especially popular during exam years in Ireland.

Can I book a tutor on this page?+

Not yet. Grindwise is currently a website prototype. The tutor profiles are examples, and the request form is a local demonstration. Accounts, messaging, payments and bookings are not available.

Are the prices and earnings guaranteed?+

No. Profile prices are illustrative, and the calculator estimates gross earnings from the hours and rate you choose. Actual rates, bookings, fees and tax will vary.

Which subjects can I explore?+

Explore school subjects, college topics, coding, languages, music and art above. Search or filter by category to find your starting point.
